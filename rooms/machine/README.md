# The Machine Room

The seventh room in **A House in Conversation**: the room the others are made of.

Everything in the house grows from one four-note seed — E–B–F♯–A. Here that seed is not an atmosphere but a mechanism: four notes standing on a lattice of scale degrees, a rotor that returns to each of them every 2.4 seconds, and a cold pluck when it arrives. The room has no duration and no ending. It runs.

The four notes can be moved. Take one with a pointer and drag it along its spoke, or choose it with the left and right arrow keys and raise or lower it with up and down. The seed is written to local browser storage as you change it, and the threshold reads it on the next visit: the house sings what you left behind, not what it was given.

This is the only room that alters another. It is also the only one that can be undone — `let the house forget` restores the original seed and removes the stored key entirely, so nothing of the change remains.

## What the change reaches

The threshold's live generative voices follow the altered seed. The fixed renders do not, and cannot: the Garden's melody, the Listening Room's stems, and the threshold's mobile fallback track are finished recordings of the original four notes. A visitor who alters the seed will hear the house disagree with itself — the entrance in one tuning, the rooms behind it in another. That disagreement is the honest shape of a house made partly of recordings.

## What the room shows

- `seed` — the four notes as they currently stand
- `state` — whether they are as the house wrote them or altered by you
- `loop` — the length of one rotation
- `traces` — how many rooms have left a trace in this browser

Nothing is transmitted, measured, or optimized. The counts are local browser storage read back to the visitor who made them.

## Run

Open `index.html` or serve the repository root and visit `/rooms/machine/`.
