# The Afterimage

The fifth room in **A House in Conversation**: a 92-second sound room that asks for no interaction beyond time.

Filtered air, two low harmonic planes, an unstable pulse, and short glitches open a field around one spoken German sentence. The field then withdraws into several seconds of visual and acoustic absence, which are part of the piece and not its end screen.

Unlike the other rooms, the Afterimage carries no fixed render. It is composed entirely in Web Audio at runtime, so the room is slightly different on every machine that plays it; the sentence is spoken by the browser's own voice where speech synthesis exists. There is no score, target state, analytics, or external service.

At the threshold the room lends the house its dimmest field — dun and slate at low strength — and pulls the entrance's filter further down than any other room. It is the quietest thing the house shows before you have entered it.

## Ending

> Manche Räume verändern nicht sich. Sie verändern uns.
> *Some rooms do not change. They change us.*

## Run

Open `index.html` or serve the repository root and visit `/rooms/afterimage/`.
