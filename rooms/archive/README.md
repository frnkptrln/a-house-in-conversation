# The Archive

The eighth room in **A House in Conversation**: the room that holds what the others said, and loses it.

Nothing arrives here on its own. A room writes into the archive by being entered — walk into the Garden and three of its sentences appear in this store; walk into it again and they are restored to full strength. A visitor who has entered nothing finds an empty room, and is told so.

Fragments surface by themselves, drift, occasionally reach for one another with a thin line, and go. Each time one is read it comes back fainter, and a fragment that has faded past the point of legibility is deleted from storage for good. Holding one in view costs it more than letting it pass: attention is the only thing that happens in this room, and it is what spends the room.

The room does not keep an even pace while this happens. The emptier it gets, the less it can afford to wait: fragments arrive faster and fainter as the store thins. When only one is left it stops overlapping anything, holds for far longer than any fragment before it, and takes more than twice as long to go.

So the archive can empty. When the last fragment is gone it says the only sentence of its own it has:

> Nothing is kept here.
> The rooms will write it again if you go back to them.

## Why it forgets

An archive that only accumulates becomes a profile: a stable description of a visitor, assembled without their asking. This one is built so that no such description can settle. It cannot rank, total, or date anything; it can only surface a sentence and lose a little of it. The house remembers less about you each time you look.

The store is a local browser key, `house-archive`, holding which rooms have written and how much of each fragment is left. It is never transmitted, and the room reads no other memory than the visit traces the rooms already keep.

## Sound

A low bed, and one long note per surfacing fragment, quieter the fainter the fragment is. The notes are drawn from the house's current seed — so if the Machine Room has altered it, the Archive reads the old rooms' words in the new tuning.

## Run

Open `index.html` or serve the repository root and visit `/rooms/archive/`.
