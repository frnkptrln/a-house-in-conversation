# The Colour Room

A bright, ninety-six-second audiovisual room inside **A House in Conversation**.

Two autonomous colour fields approach one another. Touch draws both toward the visitor and produces a third field neither contained alone. Repeated intervention lowers clarity and muddies the room; stillness gradually restores differentiation. There are no scores, instructions, or optimal outcomes.

## The flood

At forty-four and a half seconds the fields are as close as they will get, and the room does not let the moment pass politely. The third colour gathers at the point where they touch, breaks over everything, stands for about a second and a half, and drains away over three.

What it leaves does not wash out. The paper keeps the tint, both fields carry a quarter of the colour neither of them held, and their saturation never fully returns. Everything else in this room is reversible — muddying and clearing, approach and retreat — which is exactly why one irreversible thing had to happen in it. A room where nothing can be lost is a room where nothing is at stake.

## Run

Open `index.html` or serve the repository root with a static web server and visit `/rooms/colour/`.

## Sound

`audio/colour.m4a` is an original fixed composition built from pure tones, filtered air, spatial asymmetry, and three slowly arriving high voices. It is deliberately brighter than the score of the Conversation Room.
